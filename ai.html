Whole matches were held out for testing. Training uses English ASR/translated transcript windows aligned to event timestamps. These labels have not been manually verified. This model classifies text; browser speech recognition is separate. It does not detect raw crowd sounds or whistles. Physical-phone and accessibility-user testing remain outstanding.
