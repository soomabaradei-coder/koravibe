The Challenge التحدّي

Everyone is in the stadium.
Not everyone is in the match.

Millions of fans attend, but they don't experience the game equally. A shared roar can still leave people out.

Blind & low-vision fans may miss the visual action
Deaf & hard-of-hearing fans may miss the stadium sound
International fans may miss the commentary

The Solution الحل

One agent. Every sense. Connected to the live match.

KoraVibe links to the stadium's live match data and delivers the game across six channels at once — then learns what each fan actually needs.

01

Live audio description

A running voice describes the play as it happens, so the action is never invisible.

02

Spatial sound

Directional audio shows where the play is moving across the pitch — left, right, near, far.

03

Vibration & haptics

Distinct patterns pulse for a shot, a save, a goal — the moment felt through the skin.

04

Live captions

Every call and stadium cue rendered as clean, real-time text on screen.

05

Sign language

An on-screen signer relays the commentary for fans who read the game in sign.

06

Multilingual commentary

Commentary in the fan's language — with simple explanations for newcomers and tactical depth for veterans.
